# Signed maximum-index selector (`origen_189240`)

Inputs `out1` through `out10` are ten signed 26-bit two's-complement values. Registered-style output `max[3:0]` is driven combinationally. There are no clocks, resets, or parameters.

The module selects the index of the greatest signed input, encoding `out1` through `out10` as 0 through 9. Each candidate is tested with `>=` against all other inputs in ascending input order. Therefore ties choose the lowest-numbered input: for example, a maximum shared by `out2` and `out7` produces 1. A final default drives zero, although for fully known binary inputs at least one maximum condition always succeeds. Four-state unknowns can prevent conditions from succeeding and reach that default.
