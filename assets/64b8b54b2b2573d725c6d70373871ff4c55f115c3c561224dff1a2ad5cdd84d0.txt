# CDP_ICVT_leading_sign_10_0

Input is `mantissa[9:0]`; combinational output is `rtn[3:0]`. Define `z98=(mantissa[9:8]==0)`, `z76=(mantissa[7:6]==0)`, `z54=(mantissa[5:4]==0)`, `z32=(mantissa[3:2]==0)`, `h2=z98&z76`, `h3=h2&z54&z32`, and `allz=h3&(mantissa[1:0]==0)`. Then `rtn[3]=h3`, `rtn[2]=h2&~(z54&z32)`, `rtn[1]=(z98&(z54|~z76)&~h3)|allz`, and `rtn[0]=~(mantissa[9] | ~(mantissa[8:7]!=2'b01) | ((mantissa[5] | ~(mantissa[4:3]!=2'b01))&h2) | (mantissa[1]&h3) | allz)`. Operators use normal four-state Verilog equality/logic. There is no clock, reset, state, latency, parameter, or submodule.
