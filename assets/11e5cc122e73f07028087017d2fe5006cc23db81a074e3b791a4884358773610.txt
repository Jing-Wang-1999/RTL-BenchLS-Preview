# `origen_005369` instruction control decoder

Implement a purely combinational module `origen_005369` with 6-bit inputs `op` and `func`; 2-bit outputs `RegDst`, `MemtoReg`, `ExtOp`, and `ALUOp`; and one-bit outputs `ALUSrc`, `RegWrite`, `MemRead`, `MemWrite`, `IfBeq`, `jr`, and `jal`. All outputs are `reg`.

Begin every evaluation with all outputs zero. Decode `op` as follows: `000000` sets `RegDst=01`, `RegWrite=1`, and `ALUOp=10`; within this opcode, `func=001000` additionally sets `jr=1` and clears `RegWrite`. `100011` sets `ALUSrc=1`, `MemtoReg=01`, `RegWrite=1`, `MemRead=1`, and `ExtOp=01`. `101011` sets `ALUSrc=1`, `MemWrite=1`, and `ExtOp=01`. `000100` sets `IfBeq=1`, `ALUOp=01`, and `ExtOp=01`. `001000` sets `ALUSrc=1`, `RegWrite=1`, and `ExtOp=01`. `001100` and `001101` each set `ALUSrc=1`, `RegWrite=1`, and `ALUOp=11`. `001010` sets those same three signals and `ExtOp=01`. `000010` retains every default. `000011` sets `RegDst=10`, `RegWrite=1`, `MemtoReg=10`, and `jal=1`. Every unlisted opcode retains all-zero defaults. There is no clock, reset, state, or latency.
