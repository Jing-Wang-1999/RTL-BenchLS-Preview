This module performs a signed addition with sign adjustment. It takes two 21-bit unsigned values (arg_2, arg_3) and their corresponding sign bits (arg_5, arg_6). The core operation adds the two values and, if their signs differ, increments the sum by 1. All other inputs are passed directly to outputs unchanged.

Inputs:
- Clock (clk) and reset (rst) are present but unused.
- Control signals arg_0 and arg_1.
- Two 21-bit data inputs arg_2 and arg_3.
- A 5-bit input arg_4.
- Two sign bits arg_5 and arg_6.

Outputs:
- ret_0: The 22-bit adjusted sum (21-bit sum plus possible increment).
- ret_1, ret_2: Direct copies of arg_0 and arg_1.
- ret_3: Direct copy of the 5-bit arg_4.
- ret_4, ret_5: Direct copies of the sign bits arg_5 and arg_6.

The module is purely combinational; clock and reset have no effect.
