# origen_099109

This traffic-light FSM has one-bit inputs `clk`, active-high asynchronous `rst`, `TA`, and `TB`, and active-high one-bit outputs `RA`, `YA`, `GA`, `RB`, `YB`, and `GB`. Reset selects S0. S0 shows A green/B red and remains while TA=1, otherwise goes S1. S1 shows A yellow/B red and goes to S2. S2 shows A red/B green and remains while TB=1, otherwise goes S3. S3 shows A red/B yellow and goes S0. An invalid state transitions to S0 and shows red on both roads with no yellow or green. Outputs are combinational from state. The top module instantiates no submodules; the separately defined `Traffic_sensor` is not part of its behavior.
