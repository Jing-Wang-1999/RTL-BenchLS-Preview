# FP17_ADD_mgc_shift_l_v4

Parameters are `width_a=4`, `signd_a=1`, `width_s=2`, and `width_z=8`. Inputs are `a[width_a-1:0]` and unsigned shift amount `s[width_s-1:0]`; output is `z[width_z-1:0]`. Choose extension bit `a[width_a-1]` when signd_a is nonzero and zero otherwise. Form a `max(width_a+1,width_z)`-bit vector by placing `{extension_bit,a}` in its low `width_a+1` bits and filling all higher bits with the extension bit. Shift left by s, fill low positions with zero, discard high overflow, and return the low width_z bits. The module is combinational with no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
