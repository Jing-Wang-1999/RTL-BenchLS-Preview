# `oh_oa22`

`oh_oa22` is a stateless combinational bitwise OA22 array. Parameter `DW` controls the width of inputs `a0`, `a1`, `b0`, and `b1` and output `z`; it defaults to 1. For each bit, the output is the AND of two pairwise OR results: `z = (a0 | a1) & (b0 | b1)`. There is no clock, reset, storage, or submodule.
