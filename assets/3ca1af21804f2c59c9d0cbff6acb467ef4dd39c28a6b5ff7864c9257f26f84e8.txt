# Mixed-sign multiplier (`mg_02453`)

Inputs `in0[7:0]` and `in1[7:0]` and output `out[15:0]` are declared as signed nets after their port declarations. There are no parameters, clock, reset, or submodules. The combinational expression zero-extends `in0` to a signed positive 9-bit value and multiplies it by signed two's-complement `in1`. Thus `in0` is effectively unsigned 0 through 255, `in1` is signed -128 through 127, and `out` is the signed 16-bit product; the full possible product fits 16 bits.
