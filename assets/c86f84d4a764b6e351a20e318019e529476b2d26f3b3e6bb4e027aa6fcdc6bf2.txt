# NV_NVDLA_SDP_CORE_Y_idx_core_chn_lut_out_rsci_chn_lut_out_wait_dp

All ports are scalar. Inputs are `nvdla_core_clk`, active-low asynchronous `nvdla_core_rstn`, `chn_lut_out_rsci_oswt`, `chn_lut_out_rsci_biwt`, and `chn_lut_out_rsci_bdwt`; outputs are `chn_lut_out_rsci_bawt` and `chn_lut_out_rsci_wen_comp`. Let internal bit be bcwt. Combinationally, `bawt=biwt|bcwt` and `wen_comp=~oswt|bawt`. Reset clears bcwt; each rising edge otherwise sets `bcwt=(bcwt|biwt)&~bdwt`, giving bdwt clear priority. There are no submodules.
