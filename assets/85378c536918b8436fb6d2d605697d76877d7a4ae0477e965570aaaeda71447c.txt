**Specification: origen_144596**

This module performs a 4-bit unsigned multiplication followed by a configurable bit rotation on the result.

**Inputs:**
- **A, B:** 4-bit unsigned operands for multiplication.
- **shift:** 2-bit control signal selecting the rotation operation on the product.

**Output:**
- **OUT:** 8-bit result after multiplication and rotation.

**Behavior:**
1. Multiplies two 4-bit inputs (A and B) to produce an 8-bit product.
2. Rotates the product based on the **shift** input:
   - **00:** No rotation (output = product).
   - **01:** Rotate left by 1 bit.
   - **10:** Rotate left by 2 bits.
   - **11:** Rotate right by 1 bit.

**Sub-modules:**
- A 4-bit unsigned multiplier.
- A 8-bit barrel shifter supporting left/right rotations.

The module is purely combinational with no internal state. All operations occur in a single clock cycle.
