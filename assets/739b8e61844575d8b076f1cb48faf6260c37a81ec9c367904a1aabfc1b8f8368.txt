# Two-input multiplexer (`rtlpp_02734`)

Scalar inputs are `D0`, `D1`, and select `S0`; scalar output is `O`. There are no parameters, clock, reset, or submodules. The combinational function is `O=(~S0 & D0) | (S0 & D1)`: logic-zero `S0` selects `D0`, and logic-one selects `D1`. Four-state unknowns propagate according to gate-level Verilog semantics.
