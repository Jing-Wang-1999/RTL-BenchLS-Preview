# `SDP_X_X_alu_core_staller`
All ports are scalar. Inputs are `nvdla_core_clk`, active-low asynchronous reset `nvdla_core_rstn`, `chn_alu_in_rsci_wen_comp`, `chn_alu_op_rsci_wen_comp`, and `chn_alu_out_rsci_wen_comp`. Outputs are combinational `core_wen` and registered `core_wten`.

At all times, `core_wen` is the AND of the three channel `wen_comp` inputs. A falling edge of `nvdla_core_rstn` asynchronously sets `core_wten` to 0. On every rising edge of `nvdla_core_clk` while reset is high, `core_wten` captures `~core_wen`. Consequently `core_wten` reflects the inverse readiness sampled at the preceding active clock edge; it is not a combinational inverse of the current `core_wen`. No submodule is required.
