# Specification

`mg_00462` is combinational with `sample[7:0]`, `volume[5:0]`, and `out[13:0]`. It sign-extends sample to its 14-bit two's-complement bit pattern, zero-extends volume, multiplies the two 14-bit vectors, and returns the low 14 product bits. No saturation or rounding occurs. The output port is not declared signed, although its bit pattern is the 14-bit modular signed product. There are no parameters, clock, reset, state, or submodules.
