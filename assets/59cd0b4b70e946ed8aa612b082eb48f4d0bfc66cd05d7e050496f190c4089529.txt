# Two-state FSM (`autovcoder_108528`)

Inputs are scalar `clk`, active-low asynchronous `rstn`, and `a`; registered-style combinational output is scalar `out`. There are no parameters. States are `S0=2'b00` and `S1=2'b01`.

Pulling `rstn` low immediately selects S0. At a rising edge otherwise, input zero selects S0 and input one selects S1 from either legal state. An illegal state computes S0 as the next state. `out` is combinationally zero in S0, one in S1, and unknown for an illegal state; it therefore reflects whether the last sampled `a` was one after a legal clocked update.
