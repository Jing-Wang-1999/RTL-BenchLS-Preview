# Specification

`origen_184467` has `CLK`, active-low asynchronous `RST_n`, `preset`, `EN_LEFT`, `REF_DATA[7:0]`, and eight 8-bit outputs `REF_LEFT0` through `REF_LEFT7`. Reset clears all eight stored words and the 3-bit index. On each rising edge, preset has priority and resets only the index. Otherwise EN_LEFT writes REF_DATA to the indexed word and increments the index modulo eight. When EN_LEFT is low, the index resets to zero and stored words hold. Outputs continuously expose the eight words.
