# HLS_fp17_add_core_chn_a_rsci_chn_a_wait_ctrl

All ports are scalar. Inputs are `nvdla_core_clk`, active-low asynchronous `nvdla_core_rstn`, `chn_a_rsci_oswt`, `core_wen`, `chn_a_rsci_iswt0`, `chn_a_rsci_ld_core_psct`, `core_wten`, and `chn_a_rsci_vd`; outputs are `chn_a_rsci_biwt`, `chn_a_rsci_bdwt`, and `chn_a_rsci_ld_core_sct`. Define `pdswt0=~core_wten&iswt0`, `ogwt=pdswt0|icwt`, `biwt=ogwt&vd`, `bdwt=oswt&core_wen`, and `ld_core_sct=ld_core_psct&ogwt`. Reset clears `icwt`; each rising edge otherwise sets `icwt=(icwt|pdswt0)&~biwt`. There are no submodules.
