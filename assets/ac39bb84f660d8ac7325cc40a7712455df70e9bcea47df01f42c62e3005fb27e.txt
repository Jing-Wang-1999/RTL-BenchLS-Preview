# origen_131378

Inputs are `clk`, `clk_ena`, serial `in_stream`, and active-high asynchronous `reset`. Outputs `pipeline_reg_0` through `pipeline_reg_16` are scalar views of a 17-bit register. Reset immediately clears all stages. On each rising clock edge with reset low and `clk_ena=1`, stage 0 captures `in_stream` and each stage 1–16 captures the previous lower stage (`pipeline <= {pipeline[15:0],in_stream}`). With `clk_ena=0`, all stages hold. The newest bit appears at output 0 after the accepting edge and reaches output 16 after 16 further enabled edges. No submodule exists.
