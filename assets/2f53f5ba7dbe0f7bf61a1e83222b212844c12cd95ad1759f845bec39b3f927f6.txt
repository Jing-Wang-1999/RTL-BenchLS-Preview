# Dual registered unsigned multipliers (`mg_00843`)

Inputs are scalar `clk` and unsigned 8-bit operand pairs `a0,b0` and `a1,b1`. Outputs `z0[15:0]` and `z1[15:0]` are registered. There are no parameters, reset, enable, or submodules. At every rising clock edge, `z0` captures the full unsigned product `a0*b0` and independently `z1` captures `a1*b1`. Each output holds that sampled product until the next rising edge; initial values are unspecified.
