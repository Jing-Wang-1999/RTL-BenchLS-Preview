# Enabled 2-to-4 decoder (`dec2to4`)

Inputs are `W[1:0]` and scalar `En`. Output `Y[0:3]` is declared with ascending indices. There are no parameters, clock, or reset. When `{En,W}` is 100, 101, 110, or 111, the assigned vector literal is respectively `1000`, `0100`, `0010`, or `0001`; because `Y` is indexed `[0:3]`, this means `W=0..3` asserts `Y[0]..Y[3]`. When disabled or when the selector contains an unmatched four-state value, all four outputs are zero.
