The SDP_X_X_alu_core_staller module is designed to manage the stalling mechanism for an arithmetic logic unit (ALU) within a system. It operates based on clock and reset signals, and controls the enable signals for the ALU's input, operation, and output channels.

**Inputs:**
1. `nvdla_core_clk`: The clock signal for synchronization.
2. `nvdla_core_rstn`: The reset signal, active low, used to initialize the module.
3. `chn_alu_in_rsci_wen_comp`: A signal indicating whether the ALU input channel is ready.
4. `chn_alu_op_rsci_wen_comp`: A signal indicating whether the ALU operation channel is ready.
5. `chn_alu_out_rsci_wen_comp`: A signal indicating whether the ALU output channel is ready.

**Outputs:**
1. `core_wen`: A signal that enables the core operation when all input, operation, and output channels are ready.
2. `core_wten`: A signal that indicates whether the core should be stalled. It is the inverse of `core_wen`.

**Behavior:**
- The module combines the readiness signals from the input, operation, and output channels using a logical AND operation to generate the `core_wen
