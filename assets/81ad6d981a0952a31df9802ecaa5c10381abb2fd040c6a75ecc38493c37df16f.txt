# Six-way paired-signal multiplexer (`origen_167265`)

Input `sel[2:0]` selects among scalar inputs `out_ack_reset_0` through `_5` and corresponding scalar `packet_drop_signal_0` through `_5`. Outputs are scalar `out_ack_reset_out` and `packet_drop_signal_out`. There are no parameters, clock, reset, or submodules.

For selector values zero through five, both outputs combinationally take the pair having that numeric suffix. Selector values six and seven, and unmatched four-state selector values, drive both outputs zero.
