# `compress_32`

`compress_32` is a stateless combinational bitwise 3:2 compressor. Parameter `WIDTH` controls the width of inputs `a`, `b`, and `c` and outputs `oo` and `ot`; it defaults to 16. At each bit position, `oo` is `a XOR b XOR c`, and `ot` is `(a AND b) OR (a AND c) OR (b AND c)`. Thus `oo` is the sum bit and `ot` is the unshifted majority/carry bit for each independent position. The module does not propagate carries between positions and has no clock, reset, storage, or submodule.
