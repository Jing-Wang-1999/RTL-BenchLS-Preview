# `oh_oai222`

`oh_oai222` is a combinational bitwise OAI222 array. Parameter `DW` determines the width of six inputs `a0`, `a1`, `b0`, `b1`, `c0`, and `c1` and output `z`; it defaults to 1. Each result bit is `z = ~((a0 | a1) & (b0 | b1) & (c0 | c1))`. The module has no clock, reset, state, or submodules.
