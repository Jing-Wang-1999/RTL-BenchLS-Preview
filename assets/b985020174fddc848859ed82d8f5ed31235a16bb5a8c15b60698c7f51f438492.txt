# Specification

`mg_01101` has output `result[lpm_width-1:0]` and parameters `lpm_width=1`, `lpm_cvalue=0`, `lpm_strength="UNUSED"`, `lpm_type="lpm_constant"`, and `lpm_hint="UNUSED"`. At initialization, `lpm_width<=0` prints an error and ends simulation; otherwise a 33-bit internal value is initialized from `lpm_cvalue`. `result` continuously equals bits `lpm_width-1:0` of that value. The last three parameters do not affect behavior. Practical `lpm_width` must be 1 through 33 because the stored value is 33 bits.
