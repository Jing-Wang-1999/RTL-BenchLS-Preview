# autovcoder_068314

This positive-edge pipeline register has one-bit inputs `clk`, active-high synchronous `reset`, `write`, `RegWrite_in`, `MemtoReg_in`, `MemRead_in`, `MemWrite_in`, `ALUSrc_in`, and `Branch_in`; `ALUop_in[1:0]`; `pc_in`, `ALU_A_in`, `ALU_B_in`, and `imm_in[31:0]`; `funct7_in[6:0]`; `funct3_in[2:0]`; and `rs1_in`, `rs2_in`, `rd_in[4:0]`. It has same-named `_out` ports of matching widths for every payload/control after clk/reset/write.

On each rising edge, reset clears every output to zero. Otherwise write=1 captures all inputs into their corresponding outputs simultaneously; write=0 holds every output. Reset has priority. There are no submodules.
