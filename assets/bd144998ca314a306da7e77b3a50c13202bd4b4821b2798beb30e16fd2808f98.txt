### Module Specification: autovcoder_017920

**Overview**  
This module is a combinatorial logic circuit with six inputs and one output. It computes a specific boolean function and includes precise timing characteristics for signal propagation.

**Interface**  
- **Inputs:** IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4, IN5, IN6 (each 1-bit digital signals).  
- **Output:** OUT (1-bit digital signal).  
All signals operate at standard logic levels (0 or 1).

**Functionality**  
The output is the logical inversion of a three-term AND operation. The three terms are derived as follows:  
1. Term A: Logical OR of IN5 and IN6.  
2. Term B: Logical OR of IN3 and IN4.  
3. Term C: Logical OR of IN1 and IN2.  
The output is asserted low (0) only when all three terms (A, B, C) are high (1). Otherwise, the output is high (1).  
In summary: OUT = NOT ( (IN5 OR IN6) AND (IN3 OR IN4) AND (IN1 OR IN2) ).

**Timing Requirements**  
The module specifies propagation delays from each input to the output:  
- **Rising edge delay (low-to-high transition at OUT):**  
  - IN1: 0.26 time units  
  - IN2: 0.30  
  - IN3: 0.33  
  - IN4: 0.29  
  - IN5: 0.30  
  - IN6: 0.34  
- **Falling edge delay (high-to-low transition at OUT):**  
  - IN1: 0.27  
  - IN2: 0.28  
  - IN3: 0.37  
  - IN4: 0.37  
  - IN5: 0.41  
  - IN6: 0.41  
Delays are fixed (min:typ:max values are identical). Timing must be considered for critical path analysis.

**Allowed Implementation**  
The design must use only basic logic gates: OR gates (2-input), AND gates (3-input), and an inverter (NOT). No sequential elements (flip-flops, latches) or other sub-modules are permitted.

**Notes**  
This is a purely combinatorial block with no internal state. Output depends solely on current input values. Timing specifications are essential for integration into larger systems with delay constraints.
