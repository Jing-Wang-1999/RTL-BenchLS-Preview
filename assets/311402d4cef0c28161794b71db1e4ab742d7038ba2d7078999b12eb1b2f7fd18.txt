# Opcode decoder (`origen_173586`)

Input is `opCode[5:0]`. Scalar combinational outputs are `regDst`, `aluSrc`, `memToReg`, `regWrite`, `memRead`, `memWrite`, `branch`, and `jmp`; `aluOp` is two bits. There are no parameters, clocks, resets, or submodules.

Opcode 02 (jump) sets only jmp. Opcode 00 (R-type) sets regDst, regWrite, and aluOp=2. Opcode 23 (load) sets aluSrc, memToReg, regWrite, memRead, aluOp=0. Opcode 2b (store) sets aluSrc and memWrite. Opcode 04 (branch) sets branch and aluOp=1. Opcode 08 (addi) sets aluSrc, regWrite, aluOp=3. Every unlisted opcode drives all scalar outputs zero and aluOp zero. Outputs update combinationally.
