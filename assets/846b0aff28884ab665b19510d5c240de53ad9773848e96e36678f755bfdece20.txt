# Ten-state terminal FSM (`origen_034521`)

Inputs are scalar `clk`, active-low asynchronous `resetn`, `x`, and `y`; combinational outputs are scalar `f` and `g`. State parameter defaults are IDLE=0, F0=1, F1=2, X0=3, X1=4, X2=5, Y0=6, Y1=7, Y00=8, Y01=9.

Reset immediately selects IDLE. Rising-edge transitions are IDLE→F0→F1; F1 goes X0 on x else holds; X0 holds on x else goes X1; X1 goes X2 on x else F1; X2 goes Y1 on y else Y0; Y0 goes Y01 on y else Y00. Y1, Y00, and Y01 hold forever until reset. An illegal state recovers to IDLE. `f` is high only in F0. `g` is high in X2, Y0, Y1, and Y01, and low otherwise.
