# Module Specification: mg_00462

This module performs audio sample amplification based on a volume control parameter.

**Inputs:**
- `sample`: 8-bit signed audio sample
- `volume`: 6-bit unsigned volume control value

**Outputs:**
- `out`: 14-bit signed result
