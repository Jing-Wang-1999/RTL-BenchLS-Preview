This module is a parameterized AOI222 gate array. It takes six DW-bit inputs grouped as three pairs: (a0,a1), (b0,b1), (c0,c1). For each bit position, it computes the logical AND of each pair, then the OR of those three AND results, and finally outputs the bitwise logical NOT of that result. The output z is a DW-bit signal. No internal sub-modules are used; it is a pure combinational function.
