# `SDP_X_X_relu_core_cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_obj_cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_wait_ctrl`

This stateless combinational module has four one-bit inputs and two one-bit outputs. It drives `cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_obj_biwt` high exactly when `core_wten` is low and `cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_obj_iswt0` is high. It drives `cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_obj_bdwt` high exactly when both `cfg_relu_bypass_rsc_triosy_obj_oswt` and `core_wen` are high. There is no clock, reset, storage, latency, priority, or submodule.
