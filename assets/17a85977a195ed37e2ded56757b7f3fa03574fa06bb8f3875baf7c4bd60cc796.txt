The module mg_03407 is a synchronous reset circuit. It has two inputs: a clock signal (clk) and an active-low reset signal (in_rst_n). Upon receiving a negative edge on in_rst_n, the module resets its internal 2-bit register (ff) to zero. On each positive clock edge, if not in reset, the most significant bit of ff is set to one, while the least significant bit takes the value of the most significant bit from the previous clock cycle. The output (out_rst_n) reflects the value of the least significant bit of ff, indicating the reset state.
