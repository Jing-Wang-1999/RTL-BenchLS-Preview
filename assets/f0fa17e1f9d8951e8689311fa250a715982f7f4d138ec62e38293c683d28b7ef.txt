# gf_mult_by_a7

Input `i[7:0]` and output `o[7:0]` are combinational. Output bits 0 through 7 XOR input-index sets `{0,1,3,5,7}`, `{0,1,2,4,6}`, `{0,2}`, `{5,7}`, `{1,3,5,6,7}`, `{0,2,4,6,7}`, `{1,3,5,7}`, and `{0,2,4,6}` respectively. There is no parameter, clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
