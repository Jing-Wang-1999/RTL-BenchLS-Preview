# mg_05229

Inputs are scalar `s1`, `s2`, `c1`, `c2`, and `CLK`, plus `d1[15:0]` and `d2[15:0]`. Registered outputs are `od[15:0]`, `os`, and `oc`. On each rising CLK edge, s1 has priority: s1 selects d1 and sets os; otherwise s2 selects d2 and sets os; neither selects zero and clears os. Independently, oc captures `c1|c2`. There is no reset, enable, hold case, parameter, or submodule; initial output values are unspecified.
