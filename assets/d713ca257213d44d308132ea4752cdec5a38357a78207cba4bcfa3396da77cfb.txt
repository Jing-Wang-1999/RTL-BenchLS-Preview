# FP17_TO_FP16_mgc_shift_r_v4

Parameters `width_a=4`, `signd_a=1`, `width_s=2`, and `width_z=8`. Inputs are `a[width_a-1:0]`, unsigned `s[width_s-1:0]`; output is `z[width_z-1:0]`. Extend a to `max(signd_a?width_a:width_a+1,width_z)` bits using a's MSB in signed mode and zero otherwise, arithmetically shift right by s, and return the low width_z bits. Counts beyond the working width yield all sign bits or zeros. No clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
