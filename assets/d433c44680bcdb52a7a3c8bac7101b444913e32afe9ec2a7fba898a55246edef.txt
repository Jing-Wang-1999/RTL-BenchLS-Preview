# FP16_TO_FP17_mgc_in_wire_wait_v1

Parameters are integer `rscid=1` and integer `width=8`. Inputs are scalar `ld`, scalar `vz`, and `z[width-1:0]`; outputs are scalar `vd`, scalar `lz`, and `d[width-1:0]`. Combinationally and without gating, `d=z`, `lz=ld`, and `vd=vz`. In particular, `d` follows `z` even when `ld` or `vz` is low; the signals do not implement storage or conditional transfer. `rscid` does not affect behavior. There is no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
