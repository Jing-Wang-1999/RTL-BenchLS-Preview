3. **DATA**: In this state, the module continues to capture the next 8 bits of data, shifting each bit into the shift register as it arrives.

4. **PARITY**: The module captures the parity bit, which is used to verify the integrity of the received data.

5. **STOP**: The final state checks the stop bit and verifies the parity of the received data.
