# autovcoder_103973

This combinational 16-bit variable left shifter has inputs `x[15:0]`, shift amount `b[3:0]`, and mode bit `alufn`, with output `shiftOutput[15:0]`. There are no parameters or submodules.

Four cascaded stages conditionally shift left by 8, 4, 2, and 1 positions according to `b[3]`, `b[2]`, `b[1]`, and `b[0]`. Thus the total shift distance is the unsigned value of `b`. Bits shifted above bit 15 are discarded. Vacated low bits are filled with one only when both `alufn=1` and the original `x[0]=1`; otherwise they are filled with zero. When `b=0`, the output equals `x`. The module has no clock, reset, state, or latency.
