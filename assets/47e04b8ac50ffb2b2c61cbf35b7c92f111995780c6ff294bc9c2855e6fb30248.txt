# `oh_nand4`

`oh_nand4` is a combinational, bitwise four-input NAND array. Parameter `DW` sets the width of inputs `a`, `b`, `c`, and `d` and output `z`; its default value is 1. For every bit position, `z` is the inverse of the AND of the corresponding four input bits: `z = ~(a & b & c & d)`. The module has no clock, reset, state, or submodules.
