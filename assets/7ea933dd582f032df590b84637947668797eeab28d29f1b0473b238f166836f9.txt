# Valid-choice inverter (`mg_00492`)

Scalar inputs are `io_valid_0` and unused `io_ready`; scalar output is `io_chosen`. There are no parameters, clock, reset, state, or submodules. `io_chosen` is combinationally zero when `io_valid_0` is one and one otherwise, equivalent to logical inversion for known binary inputs. `io_ready` has no effect.
