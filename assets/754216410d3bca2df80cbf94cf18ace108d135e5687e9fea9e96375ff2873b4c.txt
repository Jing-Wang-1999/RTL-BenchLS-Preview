# `UINT16_TO_FP17_mgc_in_wire_wait_v1`

This stateless combinational handshake adapter has parameters `rscid`, default 1, and `width`, default 8. Inputs are one-bit `ld` and `vz` and `width`-bit `z`; outputs are one-bit `lz` and `vd` and `width`-bit `d`. It continuously assigns `d = z`, `lz = ld`, and `vd = vz`. The signals do not gate one another, and `rscid` does not affect behavior. There is no clock, reset, storage, or latency.
