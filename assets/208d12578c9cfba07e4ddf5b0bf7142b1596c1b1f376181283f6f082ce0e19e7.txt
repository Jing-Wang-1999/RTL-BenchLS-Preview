# mg_04164

This serial 16-bit CRC/LFSR has one-bit inputs `clk`, active-high synchronous `reset`, `enable`, `init`, and `data_in`, and output `crc_out[15:0]`. On a rising edge, reset loads `16'hFFFF`; otherwise enable permits an update, with init loading the same seed and taking priority over data. With enable low, state holds.

For a data update, bits shift toward higher indexes. New bit 0 is `data_in ^ old[15]`; new bits 5 and 12 are respectively `old[4] ^ data_in ^ old[15]` and `old[11] ^ data_in ^ old[15]`; every other bit n receives old bit n-1. This corresponds to polynomial taps x^16+x^12+x^5+1 rather than CRC-16-IBM. `crc_out` continuously exposes the state. There are no submodules.
