The hardware design consists of two modules: `origen_085719` and `ADD48`. 

### Module: origen_085719

**Purpose**: This module performs 16-bit addition with carry-in and provides outputs for the sum, carry-out, and overflow detection.

**Inputs**:
- `A`: A 16-bit input operand.
- `B`: A 16-bit input operand.
- `CI`: A single-bit carry-in input.

**Outputs**:
- `S`: A 16-bit output representing the sum of inputs `A`, `B`, and `CI`.
- `CO`: A single-bit output representing the carry-out from the addition.
- `OFL`: A single-bit output indicating an overflow condition. Overflow occurs if the sign bits of `A` and `B` are the same, but the sign bit of `S` differs.

**Behavior**: The module calculates the sum of `A`, `B`, and `CI`, producing a 17-bit result. The least significant 16 bits are assigned to `S`, while the most significant bit is assigned to `CO`. Overflow detection is based on the sign bits of the operands and the result.
