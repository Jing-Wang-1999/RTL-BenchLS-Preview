# Pipeline stage register (`origen_078230`)

Inputs are scalar `clockIn`, active-high asynchronous `reset`, `stall`, `memStall`, `haltIn`, `regWriteIn`, `mem2RegIn`, `memWriteIn`, and `memReadIn`; 32-bit `data1In` and `data2In`; and 5-bit `regIn` and `reg2In`. Corresponding outputs have the same widths and `Out` suffixes. There are no parameters.

Reset immediately clears every output. At a rising edge otherwise, all outputs capture their corresponding inputs only when both `stall=0` and `memStall=0`. Assertion of either stall input prevents the entire bundle from updating, so all outputs hold. Reset has priority over stalls and capture.
