- `width_a`: Specifies the bit-width of the input data `a`.
- `signd_a`: Indicates if the input data `a` is signed (1) or unsigned (0).
- `width_s`: Specifies the bit-width of the shift amount `s`.
- `width_z`: Specifies the bit-width of the output `z`.

### Output:
