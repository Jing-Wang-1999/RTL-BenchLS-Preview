# SDP_Y_INP_mgc_out_stdreg_wait_v1

This is a purely combinational pass-through module. It has integer parameters `rscid = 1` and `width = 8`; `rscid` does not affect behavior. Inputs are one-bit `ld`, one-bit `vz`, and `width`-bit `d`. Outputs are one-bit `lz`, one-bit `vd`, and `width`-bit `z`.

At all times, `z = d`, `lz = ld`, and `vd = vz`. There is no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
