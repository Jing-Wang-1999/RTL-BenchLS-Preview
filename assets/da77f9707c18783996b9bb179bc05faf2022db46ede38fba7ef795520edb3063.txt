# autovcoder_097021

This dual-edge register has parameters `ActiveLevel = 1` and `NrOfBits = 1`. Inputs are one-bit `Clock`, `ClockEnable`, active-high asynchronous `Reset`, `Tick`, active-high output-disable `cs`, active-high asynchronous preset `pre`, and data `D[NrOfBits-1:0]`; output is `Q[NrOfBits-1:0]`.

One register captures on rising Clock edges and one on falling edges. For both, Reset clears all bits with priority over pre, pre sets all bits, and otherwise the register captures D only when ClockEnable and Tick are both one; it holds otherwise. With `cs=1`, Q is all Z. With `cs=0`, nonzero ActiveLevel selects the rising-edge register and zero selects the falling-edge register. Neither register has an initial value, so reset or preset is required for known state. No submodules are used.
