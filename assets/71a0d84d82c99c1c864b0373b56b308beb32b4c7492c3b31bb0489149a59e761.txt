This module detects a specific 7-bit pattern (0101010) on a serial input stream. It operates on a clock with optional clock enable and an active-low reset.

Inputs: Reset (rst_n), clock (clk), clock enable (clken), serial data bit (j), and a secondary data bit (se0). The internal 7-bit shift register is updated on each enabled clock cycle with the logical OR of 'j' and 'se0'.
