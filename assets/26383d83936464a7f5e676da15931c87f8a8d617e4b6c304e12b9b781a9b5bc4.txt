# 16-bit adder (`origen_085719`)

Top-level inputs are unsigned vectors `A[15:0]`, `B[15:0]`, and scalar carry-in `CI`; outputs are `S[15:0]`, scalar carry-out `CO`, and signed-overflow flag `OFL`. There are no parameters, clocks, resets, or required submodules.

Combinationally `{CO,S}=A+B+CI`. `OFL` is high when A and B have equal sign bits and the result sign differs from A, which is the conventional two's-complement overflow test (the carry-in participates through S). The source file also contains an unrelated `ADD48` module that instantiates undefined vendor blocks; it is not instantiated by and is outside the `origen_085719` top contract.
