# `mg_10431` registered comparator

Inputs are unsigned `INP[7:0]`, unsigned `INN[7:0]`, `CLK`, and `PWRDN`; registered outputs are `GREATER` and `EQUAL`. Parameters are `PWRDN_SYNC=0`, `CLK_EDGE="RISING"`, and `GREATER_OR_EQUAL=0`. `PWRDN` and `PWRDN_SYNC` do not affect behavior.

Both outputs initialize to zero through Verilog initial assignments. When `CLK_EDGE` is `"RISING"`, they update on each rising edge of `CLK`; for any other value they update on each falling edge. `EQUAL` captures `(INP == INN)`. If `GREATER_OR_EQUAL=1`, `GREATER` captures `(INP >= INN)`; otherwise it captures `(INP > INN)`. There is no reset.
