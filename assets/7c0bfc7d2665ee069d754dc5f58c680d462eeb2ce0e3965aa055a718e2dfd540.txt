# Signed lane reorder (`mg_03889`)

Inputs `i_0`, `i_1`, `i_2`, and `i_3` and outputs `o_0`, `o_1`, `o_2`, and `o_3` are signed 28-bit vectors. There are no parameters, clocks, resets, state, or submodules. Continuous assignments implement `o_0=i_0`, `o_1=i_2`, `o_2=i_1`, and `o_3=i_3`; thus lanes one and two are exchanged while lanes zero and three pass through unchanged. Signed declarations do not change the bitwise pass-through behavior.
