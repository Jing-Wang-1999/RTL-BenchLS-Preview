The module takes a 64-bit input A and a 2-bit control signal factor, and produces a 64-bit output B. Based on factor, B is generated by right-shifting A and zero-extending the result. When factor is 00, B equals A (no shift). When factor is 01, B is A shifted right by 4 bits, with zeros filling the 4 most significant bits. When factor is 10, B is A shifted right by 8 bits, with zeros filling the 8 most significant bits. When factor is 11, B is A shifted right by 12 bits, with zeros filling the 12 most significant bits. The output is updated whenever A or factor changes.
