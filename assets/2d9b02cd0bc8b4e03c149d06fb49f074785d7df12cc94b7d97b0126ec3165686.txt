# `oh_mxi3`

`oh_mxi3` is combinational with parameter `DW=1`. Inputs are `d0[DW-1:0]`, `d1[DW-1:0]`, `d2[DW-1:0]`, `s0[DW-1:0]`, and `s1[DW-1:0]`; output is `z[DW-1:0]`. It has no clock, reset, or state.

`z = ~((d0 & ~s0 & ~s1) | (d1 & s0 & ~s1) | (d2 & s1))`. All operators are bitwise, so each bit selects independently: `s1` selects `d2`; otherwise `s0` selects `d1`; otherwise `d0` is selected. The selected bit is inverted.
