# `origen_038774`

`origen_038774` has inputs clk, active-high asynchronous reset, nozzleSwitch, pressureSensor; outputs fuel_out and State_out[2:0]. Reset enters 000. On rising edges: 000/001 go to 010 if both inputs are high else 001; 010/011 go to 100 if both high else 011; 100 holds; illegal states go to 000. fuel_out is one only in 001 and 011. State_out continuously reports current state. No submodule is used.
