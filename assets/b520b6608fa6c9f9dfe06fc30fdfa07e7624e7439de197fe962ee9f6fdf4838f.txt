# `gf_mult_by_d2`

`gf_mult_by_d2` is a stateless combinational module with input `i[7:0]` and output `o[7:0]`. XOR is over GF(2): `o[0]=i[1]^i[2]^i[4]^i[5]^i[7]`; `o[1]=i[0]^i[2]^i[3]^i[5]^i[6]`; `o[2]=i[2]^i[3]^i[5]^i[6]`; `o[3]=i[1]^i[2]^i[3]^i[5]^i[6]`; `o[4]=i[0]^i[1]^i[3]^i[5]^i[6]`; `o[5]=i[1]^i[2]^i[4]^i[6]^i[7]`; `o[6]=i[0]^i[2]^i[3]^i[5]^i[7]`; `o[7]=i[0]^i[1]^i[3]^i[4]^i[6]`. No clock, reset, state, or submodule is used.
