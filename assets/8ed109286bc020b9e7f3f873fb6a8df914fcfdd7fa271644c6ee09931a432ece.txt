The `servant_gpio` module is a GPIO controller interfacing with a Wishbone bus. It has four inputs: a clock signal (`i_wb_clk`), data input (`i_wb_dat`), write enable (`i_wb_we`), and cycle indicator (`i_wb_cyc`). It outputs two signals: `o_wb_rdt` and `o_gpio`.
