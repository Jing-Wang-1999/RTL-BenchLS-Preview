# `SDP_X_X_alu_core_cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_obj_cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_wait_ctrl`

This stateless combinational module has four one-bit inputs and two one-bit outputs. `cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_obj_biwt` is high exactly when `core_wten` is low and `cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_obj_iswt0` is high. `cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_obj_bdwt` is high exactly when both `cfg_alu_shift_value_rsc_triosy_obj_oswt` and `core_wen` are high. There is no clock, reset, state, priority, latency, or submodule.
