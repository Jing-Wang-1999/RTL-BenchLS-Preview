# Specification

`mg_06631` parameters are `FB_X_MAX=1280` and `FB_Y_MAX=1024`. Inputs are `clk`, unused `pixel_count[11:0]`, unused `line_count[11:0]`, synchronous active-high `fb_reset`, and `fb_enable`; outputs are registered `x[11:0]`, `y[11:0]`. Reset sets both to zero. On an enabled rising edge, x increments and wraps to zero at FB_X_MAX-1; on that same wrap, y increments and wraps at FB_Y_MAX-1. Disabled cycles hold both. Parameter values must fit the 12-bit coordinates and be positive.
