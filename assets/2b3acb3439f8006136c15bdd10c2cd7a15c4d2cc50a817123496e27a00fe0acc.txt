# ym2149_logic

Inputs are active-high asynchronous `RESET`, `CLK`, `CE`, `SEL`, unused `MODE`, `BC`, `BDIR`, and `DI[7:0]`. Outputs `CHANNEL_A`, `CHANNEL_B`, and `CHANNEL_C` are eight bits and continuously expose three internal registers.

Reset clears all channels. On a rising CLK edge outside reset, writes occur only if CE and BDIR are high. SEL=1 and BC=1 writes DI to A; SEL=1 and BC=0 writes B; SEL=0 writes C regardless of BC. Registers not selected hold. MODE has no effect. There are no submodules.
