# Decode-to-execute pipeline register (`origen_092256`)

Inputs are scalar `clk`, active-high asynchronous `reset`, and synchronous active-high `FlushE`; decode controls `RegDstD[1:0]`, `MemtoRegD`, `MemWriteD`, `ALUOpD[1:0]`, `ALUsrcD`, `RegWriteD`; addresses `RsD`, `RtD`, `RdD` each 5 bits; data `PC8D`, `RD1D`, `RD2D`, `extD` each 32 bits; and flags `cal_rD`, `cal_iD`, `ldD`, `stD`, `jalD`. Outputs are the identically sized signals with suffix E.

Asserting reset clears every output immediately. At each rising edge otherwise, `FlushE=1` clears every output; with `FlushE=0`, all outputs capture their matching D inputs. Reset has priority, followed by FlushE, followed by capture. There is no enable or hold condition.
