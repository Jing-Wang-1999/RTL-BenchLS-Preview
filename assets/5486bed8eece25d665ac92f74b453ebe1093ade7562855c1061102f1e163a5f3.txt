# Dual-edge selectable register (`autovcoder_097098`)

Parameters default to `ActiveLevel=1` and `NrOfBits=1`. Inputs are scalar `Clock`, `ClockEnable`, active-high asynchronous `Reset`, scalar `Tick`, scalar output-disable `cs`, active-high asynchronous preset `pre`, and `D[NrOfBits-1:0]`. Output is `Q[NrOfBits-1:0]`.

One register responds to the rising Clock edge and one to the falling edge. In either process, Reset immediately clears with priority over preset, preset immediately fills ones, and an applicable clock edge loads D only when both ClockEnable and Tick are high; otherwise it holds. Declaration initialization clears only the rising-edge register. When `cs=1`, Q is all high impedance. Otherwise `ActiveLevel!=0` selects the rising-edge register and zero selects the falling-edge register.
