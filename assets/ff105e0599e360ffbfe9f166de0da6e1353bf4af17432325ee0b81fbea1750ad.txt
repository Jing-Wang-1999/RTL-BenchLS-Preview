# asic_dmux3

Parameter `PROP="DEFAULT"` does not affect behavior. Scalar inputs are `sel0`, `sel1`, `sel2`, `in0`, `in1`, and `in2`; scalar output is `out`. Combinationally, `out=(sel0&in0)|(sel1&in1)|(sel2&in2)`. Multiple asserted selects OR their selected data; no priority is applied. With no select asserted, out is zero. There is no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
