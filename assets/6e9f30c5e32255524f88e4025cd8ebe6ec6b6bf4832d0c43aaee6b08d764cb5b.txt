The module is a synchronous 8-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) with registered outputs.

**Inputs:**
- `clk`: System clock.
- `rst_n`: Active-low asynchronous reset.
- `opcode` (3-bit): Selects the operation to perform.
- `dina` (8-bit): First data input operand.
- `dinb` (8-bit): Second data input operand.

**Outputs:**
- `cout` (1-bit): Carry-out flag. Only valid for the addition operation.
- `dout` (8-bit): Registered result of the selected operation.

**Behavior:**
On every positive clock edge, the module samples its inputs and computes a new result. An active-low reset asynchronously clears both `cout` and `dout` to zero.

The operation performed is determined by the `opcode`:
- `000`: Addition. Outputs the 8-bit sum of `dina` and `dinb`. `cout` is the carry-out from this addition.
- `001`: Absolute Difference. Outputs the absolute value of `dina` minus `dinb`. `cout` is cleared.
- `010`: Bitwise AND of `dina` and `dinb`. `cout` is cleared.
- `011`: Bitwise OR of `dina` and `dinb`. `cout` is cleared.
- `100`: Bitwise XOR of `dina` and `dinb`. `cout` is cleared.
- `101`: Logical Left Shift. `dina` is shifted left by the number of bits specified by the three least significant bits of `dinb`. Vacated bits are filled with zeros. `cout` is cleared.
- `110`: Logical
