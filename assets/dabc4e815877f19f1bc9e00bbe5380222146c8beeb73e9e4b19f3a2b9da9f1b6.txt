# `FP16_TO_FP17_mgc_out_stdreg_wait_v1`

This stateless combinational handshake adapter has parameters `rscid`, default 1, and `width`, default 8. Inputs are one-bit `ld` and `vz` and `width`-bit `d`; outputs are one-bit `lz` and `vd` and `width`-bit `z`. It continuously assigns `z = d`, `lz = ld`, and `vd = vz`. The signals do not gate one another, and `rscid` does not affect behavior. Despite its name, the module contains no register, clock, reset, storage, or latency.
