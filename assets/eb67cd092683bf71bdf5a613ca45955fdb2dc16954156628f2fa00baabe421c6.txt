# SDP_Y_CORE_Y_mul_core_cfg_mul_op_rsc_triosy_obj_cfg_mul_op_rsc_triosy_wait_dp

Inputs are scalar `nvdla_core_clk`, active-low asynchronous `nvdla_core_rstn`, `cfg_mul_op_rsc_triosy_obj_biwt`, and `cfg_mul_op_rsc_triosy_obj_bdwt`; output is scalar `cfg_mul_op_rsc_triosy_obj_bawt`. Let internal bit be bcwt. Combinationally, `bawt=biwt|bcwt`. Reset clears bcwt. Each rising edge otherwise sets `bcwt=(bcwt|biwt)&~bdwt`, so bdwt has clear priority over simultaneous biwt. There are no submodules.
