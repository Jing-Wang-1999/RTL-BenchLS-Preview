# FP32_ADD_mgc_in_wire_wait_v1

This purely combinational pass-through has integer parameters `rscid = 1` and `width = 8`; `rscid` does not affect behavior. Inputs are one-bit `ld`, one-bit `vz`, and `width`-bit `z`. Outputs are one-bit `lz`, one-bit `vd`, and `width`-bit `d`.

At all times, `d = z`, `lz = ld`, and `vd = vz`. The data connection is unconditional and is not gated by either handshake signal. There is no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
