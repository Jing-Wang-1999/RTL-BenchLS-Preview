**4-Bit Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) Module Specification**

This module implements a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder, accelerating carry propagation for faster addition. It takes:

- **Inputs**: Carry-in (`Ci`), generate (`G0-G3`) and propagate (`P0-P3`) signals for each bit.
- **Outputs**: Carry-outs (`C1-C3`) for bits 1-3, group generate (`GG`), and group propagate (`GP`) signals.

**Behavior**:
- Computes intermediate carry signals by combining generate/propagate bits with the carry-in, eliminating ripple delays.
- `GG` indicates if the 4-bit group generates a carry, while `GP` indicates if it propagates an incoming carry.
- Allowed sub-modules: None (pure combinational logic).

**Use Case**: High-speed arithmetic operations in processors or digital systems.
