# Specification

`mg_03996` is a combinational four-input LUT. It has scalar inputs `I0` through `I3`, scalar output `O`, and parameter `LUTMASK=16'h0000`. The inputs form the LUT index with I3 most significant and I0 least significant: `O=LUTMASK[{I3,I2,I1,I0}]`. Equivalently, I3 selects an 8-bit half, I2 a 4-bit quarter, I1 a pair, and I0 one bit. It has no clock, reset, state, or submodule.
