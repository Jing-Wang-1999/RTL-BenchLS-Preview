# Specification

`mg_06480` has `clk`, active-high clock enable `ce`, signed inputs `a[31:0]`, `b[31:0]`, and registered output `p[31:0]`. The combinational product is truncated to its low 32 bits. On each rising edge with ce high, p captures that truncated product; with ce low it holds. There is no reset or initial value, so p is unspecified before the first enabled capture. There are no parameters or submodules.
