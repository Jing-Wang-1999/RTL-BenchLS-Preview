# mg_04080

This combinational pass-through has parameter `DATA_WIDTH = 1`. Inputs are unused one-bit `CLK` and `RST`, `put[DATA_WIDTH-1:0]`, one-bit `EN_put`, and one-bit `EN_get`. Outputs are `get[DATA_WIDTH-1:0]`, one-bit `RDY_put`, `RDY_get`, `modReady`, and `inverseReady`.

At all times, `get=put`, both ready outputs are one, `modReady=EN_get`, and `inverseReady=EN_put`. `CLK` and `RST` have no effect. There is no state, latency, or submodule.
