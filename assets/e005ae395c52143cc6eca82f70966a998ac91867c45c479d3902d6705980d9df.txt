# Specification

`mg_03620` has scalar inputs `D`, `CLK`, `PRESET`, registered output `Q`, and one-bit parameter `INIT=1`. Q is initialized to INIT. On an asynchronous rising edge of PRESET, Q becomes one. Otherwise, on each falling edge of CLK, Q captures D. PRESET has priority when coincident with a falling clock edge. There is no reset or submodule.
