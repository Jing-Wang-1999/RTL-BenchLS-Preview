The module manages two 10-bit stack pointers (pdlidx and pdlptr) for a push-down list (PDL) controller. It operates on a clock (clk) with synchronous reset (reset). The system state is indicated by four mutually exclusive control signals: state_alu, state_fetch, state_read, and state_write.

The 32-bit operand bus (ob) supplies new pointer values. Control inputs determine pointer updates: destpdlx loads pdlidx, destpdlp loads pdlptr, pdlcnt enables increment/decrement, srcpdlpop selects decrement, srcpdlidx and srcpdlptr enable output driving.

Outputs are two registered pointers: pdlidx and pdlptr. Two drive enables, pidrive and ppdrive, indicate when pdlidx or pdlptr outputs are valid, asserted when their respective source control is active and the state is alu, write, or fetch.

Behavior:
- On reset, both pointers clear to zero.
- pdlidx updates only in state_write when destpdlx is asserted, loading the lower 10 bits of ob.
- pdlptr updates in state_read or state_fetch:
  - In state_read: if destpdlp is not asserted and pdlcnt is asserted and srcpdlpop is not asserted, pdlptr increments by 1.
  - In state_fetch: if destpdlp is asserted, pdlptr loads lower 10 bits of ob; else if destpdlp is not asserted and pdlcnt is asserted and srcpdlpop is asserted, pdlptr decrements by 1.
- All pointer updates occur synchronously on the rising clock edge.
