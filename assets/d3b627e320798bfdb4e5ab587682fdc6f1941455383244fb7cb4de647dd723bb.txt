# Six-state Moore FSM (`autovcoder_032635`)

Inputs are scalar `clk`, active-high synchronous `reset`, and `s[3:1]`. Scalar outputs are `fr3`, `fr2`, `fr1`, and `dfr`. State parameters default to `A2=0`, `B1=1`, `B2=2`, `C1=3`, `C2=4`, and `D1=5`.

On a rising edge, reset selects A2; otherwise the machine takes its combinational next state. Transitions are A2→B1/A2 by `s[1]`; B1 and B2 prioritize `s[2]` to C1, otherwise hold their B state on `s[1]` or go A2; C1 and C2 prioritize `s[3]` to D1, otherwise hold their C state on `s[2]` or go B2; D1 holds on `s[3]` or goes C2. An illegal state produces an unknown next state. The output tuple `{fr3,fr2,fr1,dfr}` is respectively 1111, 0110, 0111, 0010, 0011, or 0000 in the listed states, and xxxx in an illegal state.
