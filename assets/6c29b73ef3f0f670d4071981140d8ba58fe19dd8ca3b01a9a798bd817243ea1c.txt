# FP17_TO_FP32_mgc_out_stdreg_wait_v1

This purely combinational module has integer parameters `rscid = 1` and `width = 8`; `rscid` does not affect behavior. Inputs are `ld`, `vz`, and `d[width-1:0]`. Outputs are `lz`, `vd`, and `z[width-1:0]`.

At all times, `z = d`, `lz = ld`, and `vd = vz`. There is no register despite the module name, and no clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
