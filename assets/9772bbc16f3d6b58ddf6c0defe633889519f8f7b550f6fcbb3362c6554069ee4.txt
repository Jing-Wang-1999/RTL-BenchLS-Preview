# Specification

`origen_084347` has `clk`, active-low asynchronous `rst`; 32-bit inputs `ALU_R_in`, `ALU_R2_in`, `instr_in`, `ctrl_in`, `b_equal_res_in`, `jump_res_in`, `RF_R2_in`, `RF_R1_in`; scalar `ALU_equal_in`; and `PC0_in[15:0]`. It has corresponding registered outputs with `_out` suffix and identical widths. Falling reset clears every output. Otherwise every rising edge captures each corresponding input. There is no enable, parameter, combinational bypass, or submodule.
