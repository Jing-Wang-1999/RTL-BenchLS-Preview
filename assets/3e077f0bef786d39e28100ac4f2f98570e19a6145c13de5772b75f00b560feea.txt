This module is a simple buffer with delay stages. It has one input, A, and one output, X. The module introduces delay through two internal stages, stage1 and stage2, which sequentially pass the input signal A to the output X. It requires power supply inputs VPWR and VGND, as well as body bias inputs VPB and VNB. The module does not perform any logic operations other than signal propagation and delay. It is designed to ensure signal integrity and timing adjustments in digital circuits.
