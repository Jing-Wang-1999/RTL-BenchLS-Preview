# Specification

`origen_006185` has `clk`, active-low asynchronous `rst_n`, `opcode[2:0]`, `dina[7:0]`, `dinb[7:0]`, and registered outputs `cout`, `dout[7:0]`. Reset clears outputs. Each rising edge performs: 000 unsigned addition with carry; 001 unsigned absolute difference; 010 AND; 011 OR; 100 XOR; 101 logical left shift by dinb[2:0]; 110 logical right shift by dinb[2:0]; 111 clears both outputs. Non-add operations clear cout.
