# soc2nvdla_time_gen

Inputs are `clk` and active-low asynchronous `reset_`. Outputs are `soc2nvdla_time1_gray[28:0]`, `soc2nvdla_time0_gray[26:0]`, and scalar `soc2nvdla_time_tick`.

The two binary registers form one concatenated 56-bit counter `{time1_bin,time0_bin}` that resets to zero and increments modulo 2^56 on every rising edge. The complete 56-bit Gray code is `counter ^ (counter >> 1)` and is split into the 29-bit time1 and 27-bit time0 outputs. This is a Gray encoding of the combined counter; the high portion is not independently encoded at the boundary. `soc2nvdla_time_tick` equals `(counter >> 1)[4]`, equivalently binary counter bit 5, so it is a square-wave level that toggles every 32 clocks. There are no submodules; `Bin2Gray` is an internal function.
