# Clock-enabled binary-to-BCD converter

Implement `binary_to_bcd_converter_b` with parameters BITS_IN_PP=16,
BCD_DIGITS_OUT_PP=5 and BIT_COUNT_WIDTH_PP=4. Inputs clk_i, ce_i, rst_i and start_i
are one bit; dat_binary_i has BITS_IN_PP bits. Output dat_bcd_o contains
BCD_DIGITS_OUT_PP packed four-bit decimal digits, least significant digit lowest;
done_o is one bit. Preserve default-parameter cycle behavior.

On each rising clk_i, synchronous active-high rst_i clears busy and dat_bcd_o.
It does not reset the binary work register, decimal work register or iteration
counter. done_o is continuously the inverse of busy: high while idle, including
after reset; it is not a completion pulse.

Outside reset, priority is: (1) start_i while previously idle captures the binary
input, clears the decimal work value and sets busy, regardless of ce_i;
(2) previously busy with ce_i and the final iteration count and start_i LOW
finishes, clears busy and writes the final adjusted/shifted decimal result;
(3) previously busy with ce_i and a nonfinal count shifts the binary work value
left one and advances the decimal conversion. Otherwise hold these registers.
The visible result retains its previous value until completion or reset.

Each decimal step shifts in the previous binary MSB. Process digits from least
to most significant: a digit 0..4 shifts left with the incoming carry and emits
carry zero; a digit 5..15 subtracts five, shifts the low three bits left with the
incoming carry, and emits carry one to the next digit. Discard final carry.
The iteration counter is a separate register: on every clock, if previously idle
set it to zero; otherwise increment when ce_i and the count is not BITS_IN_PP-1.
This counter rule is not reset-gated. A continuously high start_i at the final
count prevents completion; start_i during nonfinal busy cycles does not restart.
