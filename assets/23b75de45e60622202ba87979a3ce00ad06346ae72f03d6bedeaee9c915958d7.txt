# origen_027756

This is a bank of positive-edge-triggered pipeline registers with clock `clk` and one-bit active-high stall input `stall`. Input/output field pairs are `OPin/OPout[5:0]`, `FUNCTin/FUNCTout[5:0]`, `A1in/A1out[4:0]`, `A2in/A2out[4:0]`, `RDin/RDout[4:0]`, `IMMin/IMMout[15:0]`, `ADin/ADout[25:0]`, `PCADD4in/PCADD4out[31:0]`, and `PCin/PCout[31:0]`.

All outputs initialize to zero in simulation. On each rising edge of `clk`, when `stall=0`, every output captures its corresponding input simultaneously. When `stall=1`, all outputs hold their prior values. There is no reset, parameter, or submodule.
