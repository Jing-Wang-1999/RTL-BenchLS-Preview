# origen_029234

Inputs are `ALUOp[1:0]` and `Funct[5:0]`; output `ALUOperation[3:0]` is combinational. ALUOp 00 produces ADD `0010`; 01 produces SUB `0110`. For ALUOp 10, Funct values `100000,100010,100100,100101,101010,000000,000010,010000,010010` produce respectively ADD `0010`, SUB `0110`, AND `0000`, OR `0001`, SLT `0111`, SLL `1000`, SRL `1001`, MFHI `1010`, and MFLO `1011`. Any other Funct under ALUOp 10, and ALUOp 11, produce `xxxx`. The named function and operation constants are parameters with exactly those defaults. There is no clock, reset, state, or submodule.
