# Signed low-product unit (`origen_107873`)

The top module has unsigned-bit-vector ports `A[63:0]` and `B[63:0]`, interpreted internally as signed two's-complement operands, and output `mult_low[63:0]`. There are no parameters, clocks, resets, or required submodules.

Combinational logic computes the full signed 128-bit product of `$signed(A)` and `$signed(B)` and continuously outputs its low 64 bits. Overflow beyond bit 63 is discarded; the low 64-bit result is the product modulo `2^64`, independent of signed interpretation at the output. The file also contains separate `shifter` and simulation-testbench modules, but they are not instantiated by or part of the `origen_107873` top-level contract.
