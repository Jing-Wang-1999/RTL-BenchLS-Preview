# gf_mult_by_98

Input `i[7:0]` and output `o[7:0]` form a combinational GF(2) transform. o[0] is XOR of i[1,4,6,7], o[1] is XOR of i[2,5,7], o[2] is XOR of i[1,3,4,7], o[3] is XOR of i[0,1,2,5,6,7], o[4] is XOR of i[0,2,3,4], o[5] is XOR of i[1,3,4,5], o[6] is XOR of i[2,4,5,6], o[7] is XOR of i[0,3,5,6,7]. There are no parameters, clocks, resets, state, latency, or submodules.
