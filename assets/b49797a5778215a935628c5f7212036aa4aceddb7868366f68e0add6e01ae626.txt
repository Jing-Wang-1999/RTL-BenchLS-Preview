# `mg_04052` byte-mask transformer

`mg_04052` is purely combinational. Inputs are `iSRC_MASK[3:0]` and `iSRC_DATA[31:0]`; outputs are `oDEST_MASK[3:0]` and `oDEST_DATA[31:0]`. `oDEST_MASK` always equals `iSRC_MASK`.

Number the input bytes as byte 0=`iSRC_DATA[7:0]` through byte 3=`iSRC_DATA[31:24]`. The output is selected by the complete four-bit mask: `4'hF` produces `{byte0,byte1,byte2,byte3}`; `4'h3` produces `{16'h0000,byte0,byte1}`; `4'hC` produces `{byte2,byte3,16'h0000}`; `4'h1` produces `{24'h000000,byte0}`; `4'h2` produces `{16'h0000,byte1,8'h00}`; and `4'h4` produces `{8'h00,byte2,16'h0000}`. Every other mask value, including `4'h8`, produces `{byte3,24'h000000}`. There is no clock, reset, state, or latency.
