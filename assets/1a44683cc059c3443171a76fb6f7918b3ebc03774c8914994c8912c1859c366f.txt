# gf_mult_by_96

Input `i[7:0]` and output `o[7:0]` are combinational. Output bits 0 through 7 XOR input-index sets `{1,4,5}`, `{0,2,5,6}`, `{0,3,4,5,6,7}`, `{6,7}`, `{0,1,4,5,7}`, `{1,2,5,6}`, `{2,3,6,7}`, and `{0,3,4,7}` respectively. There is no parameter, clock, reset, state, latency, or submodule.
