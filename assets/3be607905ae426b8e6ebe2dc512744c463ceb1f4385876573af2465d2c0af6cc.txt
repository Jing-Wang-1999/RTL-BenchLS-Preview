# `origen_034959` eight-bit accumulator

Inputs are `accin[7:0]`, carry-in `cin`, `clk`, and active-high asynchronous `clear`. Outputs are accumulator value `accout[7:0]` and combinational carry `cout`.

Continuously compute the nine-bit unsigned sum `accout + accin + cin`; its low eight bits are the next accumulator value and its high bit drives `cout`. On every rising edge of `clk`, the accumulator captures the low eight sum bits. If `clear` is asserted, the accumulator immediately becomes zero and clear has priority over clocked capture. Because `cout` is combinational, it always reflects the addition using the current `accout`, even while clear is asserted.
