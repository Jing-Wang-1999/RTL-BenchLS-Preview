# ycbcr_to_rgb

Inputs are `clk` and unsigned `y[7:0]`, `cb[7:0]`, `cr[7:0]`; registered outputs are `red[7:0]`, `green[7:0]`, `blue[7:0]`. There is no reset or enable.

On successive rising edges the pipeline: (1) forms signed 9-bit y-16, cb-128, cr-128; (2) multiplies adjusted Y by 149, Cr by 204 and -104, Cb by -50 and approximates 258*Cb as 256*Cb+2*Cb; (3) forms red=149Y+204Cr, green=149Y-104Cr-50Cb, blue=149Y+258Cb in signed 18-bit registers; (4) saturates each prior sum: negative to 0, bit15 or bit16 set to 255, otherwise bits [14:7]. Input-to-output latency is four rising edges; arithmetic uses the declared signed widths and may wrap before saturation. Startup is unspecified. No submodule exists.
